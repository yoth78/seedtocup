# Seed To Cup Portal

Create a premium, modern website for Seed To Cup, a coffee education and experience platform by Cashmere Events.

Brand Overview:

Seed To Cup is a signature coffee platform focused on exploring coffee from origin to consumption — bringing together coffee lovers, professionals, entrepreneurs, baristas, roasters, farmers, and industry experts. The platform combines coffee education, events, community, and consulting.

Brand tagline:

Coffee | Culture | Commerce

The website should feel premium, warm, sophisticated, and editorial — inspired by specialty coffee brands, coffee journals, and luxury hospitality. Avoid making it look like a generic event website. Use an elegant coffee-inspired aesthetic with warm neutral tones, clean typography, beautiful spacing, and strong visual storytelling.

I already have the Seed To Cup logo. Create the design so the logo can be easily added.

Website Goals:

1. Allow users to register for upcoming Seed To Cup sessions/events.

2. Allow users to purchase an annual membership giving access to all 12 sessions per year.

3. Allow users to book paid coffee consultations.

4. Showcase previous sessions/events and link to YouTube recap videos.

5. Explain the Seed To Cup story and mission.

Create the following pages:

1. HOME PAGE

Hero Section:

- Logo placement

- Headline:

  "From Seed to Cup, Discover the Story Behind Coffee"

- Subheadline:

  "A platform connecting coffee culture, knowledge, and commerce through experiences, conversations, and community."

- Buttons:

  "Register for Upcoming Session"

  "Become a Member"

  "Book a Consultation"

Sections:

- Upcoming Session preview

- What is Seed To Cup?

- Membership highlight

- Coffee Consultation highlight

- Previous Sessions preview

- YouTube recap section

- About Seed To Cup

- Footer with social media links

2. UPCOMING SESSION PAGE

Create an event registration page.

Include:

- Session title

- Date and time

- Venue

- Topic

- Speaker information

- Description

- What attendees will learn

- Registration form

Registration form fields:

- Full Name

- Email

- Phone Number

- Profession/Business

- Coffee interest category:

  - Coffee enthusiast

  - Café owner

  - Barista

  - Roaster

  - Coffee trader/exporter

  - Aspiring coffee entrepreneur

  - Other

Include payment/registration confirmation placeholder.

3. MEMBERSHIP PAGE

Create a Seed To Cup Annual Membership page.

Headline:

"Join the Seed To Cup Coffee Community"

Explain:

Members get access to all 12 Seed To Cup sessions throughout the year.

Benefits:

- Access to monthly coffee sessions

- Learning opportunities with coffee professionals

- Networking with coffee enthusiasts and businesses

- Priority access to events

- Exclusive community updates

Include:

- Annual membership pricing placeholder

- Membership registration form

- Payment placeholder

4. COFFEE CONSULTATION PAGE

Create a professional consultation booking page.

Headline:

"Expert Coffee Consultation"

Description:

"Whether you are opening a coffee shop, sourcing coffee, improving your roasting process, or exploring opportunities in the coffee industry, Seed To Cup provides guidance tailored to your needs."

Consultation topics:

- Opening a coffee shop

- Coffee business strategy

- Coffee sourcing

- Roasting guidance

- Café concept development

- Menu and product development

- General coffee industry guidance

Pricing:

30-minute consultation: 1,000 ETB

Include:

- Booking form

- Date/time selection placeholder

- Payment confirmation placeholder

5. SESSIONS / EVENTS ARCHIVE PAGE

Create a gallery showing previous Seed To Cup sessions.

Each event card should include:

- Session title

- Date

- Topic

- Image

- Short description

- Button: "Watch Recap on YouTube"

Make this section visually rich and suitable for future growth.

6. ABOUT PAGE

Create an About page explaining:

"Seed To Cup is a coffee education and experience platform created to celebrate the journey, culture, and commerce of coffee."

Include:

- Mission

- Vision

- Why Seed To Cup exists

- Connection to Cashmere Events

Technical Requirements:

- Fully responsive design for desktop and mobile.

- Create reusable components.

- Add smooth animations and transitions.

- Make forms functional with placeholder integrations.

- Create a CMS-friendly structure so future sessions/events can be added easily.

- Optimize for SEO.

- Add social media placeholders.

- Include a YouTube integration placeholder for recap videos.

Overall feeling:

Premium specialty coffee brand + educational platform + community hub.

The website should feel like a destination for anyone passionate about coffee.
